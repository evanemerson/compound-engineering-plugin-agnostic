## 2026-09-25 — PR #59 verification and residual write-back

- [ ] **P3 — A manually-merged PR never runs the sweep's Step 5 write-back, so
  its finding stays `deferred` in every sink.** Found by direct observation:
  PR #59 implemented `todos/review-2026-08-03-134500.md` finding #28 and merged
  as `62273dc` on 2026-09-13, but because it was merged by hand rather than
  through the `/cepa:sweep` pipeline, the canonical finding and its
  `memory/tasks.d/2026-08-03-fix-hang-path-executable-guard.md` copy both stayed
  open for 12 days. Closed manually this session in `8244fdd`.
  The gap is structural, not a one-off: nothing in the ordinary
  branch/PR/merge workflow closes a finding, and `/cepa:sweep` only writes back
  for items *it* consumed (Step 5 is scoped to "an item consumed from a sink").
  A finding implemented outside the pipeline is invisible to it forever.
  Candidate fixes, none chosen: (a) a sweep leg that matches merged PR bodies
  against open findings by finding-reference and reconciles on sight — the
  `/cepa:sweep` Step 2 "reconciled on sight" machinery already exists for the
  already-`completed` case and would extend; (b) a CI check that fails when a
  merged PR body cites a finding still marked `deferred`; (c) accept it and
  rely on periodic manual reconciliation.
  `file: plugins/cepa/commands/sweep.md` · `action_class: judgment`
  (which fix, and whether it is worth building, is the operator's call) ·
  `confidence: 90`
