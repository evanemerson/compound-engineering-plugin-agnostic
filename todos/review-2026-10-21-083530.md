the shard-exit-3 gap and two found `fail-fast`. One adversarial claim was
wrong in detail and is recorded as such in finding 4. The plan is gitignored,

title: Clean shards exit 3; the shard step would go red every week

**Problem:** under `set -o pipefail | tee` every clean shard fails its job;
the run never reaches `success` and the freshness detector warns forever.
**Fix:** step captures `PIPESTATUS[0]`, accepts exactly 3 (0 = filter ignored
→ fail); §9f states no step accepting 3 is a pass on its own; merge verifies
`SHARD-DONE rc=3` per shard.
