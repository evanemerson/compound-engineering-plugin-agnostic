### 1
- status: applied
- severity: P2
- agent: adversarial-reviewer, silent-failure-hunter, security-sentinel
- category: Reliability
- confidence: 70
- action_class: corroborated
- file: plugins/cepa/scripts/orca-nudge.sh
- lines: cepa_nudge_orca
- title: The `terminal send` retry could press Enter twice with no state re-check

**Problem:**
A first send that typed the line and then failed with the vsock error was retried at once. A second Enter can answer a prompt that opened in the target between the two sends (the hazard of #100 finding 3). The adversarial stub reproduced two typed lines with one `worktree ps`.

**Fix:**
Applied: before it retries `terminal send`, the wrapper re-reads the row and sends only on `done`; otherwise it skips with `<state> before the send retry`. A target whose row has not yet left `done` can still get the line twice; that limit is stated in the header. Cases `RETRY send vsock once` (asserts the extra `worktree ps`) and `RETRY send retry target left done`; mutant `retry-send-no-reread`.
