- title: Rule 8 told a session to acknowledge a peeked delivery, but `--peek` returns no delivery id

**Problem:**
Orca's `check --help` (1.4.245): `--peek` returns no deliveryId and marks nothing read. A session could not ack; the same reply came back on the next peek and could be read as the answer to a newer question. A Run coordinator improvising a plain `check` could ack a batch holding a `worker_done`.

**Fix:**
Applied: rule 8 now says a peek only shows, and a drain reads: plain `check --json`, process every message in the batch (a `worker_done` included), `check --ack <deliveryId>` until null. A reply is matched to its question by message id. A stated limit says the drain was not measured on a session with no bound Run. Mutants `peek-acks-peek`, `peek-drain-partial`, `peek-stale-reply`, `peek-drain-unmeasured-gone`.
