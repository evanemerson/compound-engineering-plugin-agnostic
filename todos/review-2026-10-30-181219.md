- lines: §12b rule 1 Send, then nudge; rule 7
- title: A skipped nudge on a busy target is skip-and-forget; the L26 lost message returns

**Problem:**
`working` is the common non-idle state. Nothing retries; rule 7's `Needed from you: nothing` depends only on the send. A coordinator busy at send time goes idle later with the question unread, and the worker has told the operator nothing is needed. The reply path fails the same way.

**Fix:**
Operator's call: (a) bounded wait on `worktree ps` until `done`, then nudge; (b) treat any skip as not delivered, so rule 7 makes it an operator line; or both.

Applied (operator chose "wait briefly, then tell me", 2026-10-30): both. A `working` target is re-read every 10 s for at most 120 s (a fixed constant), then nudged or skipped; other states skip at once. Rule 1 "Only a sent nudge completes the delivery" makes any unsent nudge an operator line (`Needed from you: tell <session> to check its Orca inbox`, message id in the body); rule 7 cites it. Cases: WAIT leg of `scripts/check-orca-nudge.sh`; controls `wait-*`, `rule-coordinator-send-only`, `rule-skip-delivered`, `nudge-wait-unbounded`, `nudge-skip-is-delivery`, `nudge-skip-no-operator-line`, `nudge-run-coord-nudged`.
