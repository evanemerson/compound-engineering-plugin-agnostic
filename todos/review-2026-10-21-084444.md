# Plan review — dispatching commands leave no agent-* worktrees

Three personas (sonnet, headless), each dispatched with the new `READ-ONLY:`
brief line and no isolation. The first dispatch attempt was DENIED by a
user-level hook (`~/.claude/hooks/require_worktree_isolation.py`) because it
carried neither `isolation: "worktree"` nor a `READ-ONLY:` line — the likely
source of the leftovers on this machine, and the reason the brief line opens
with that token. After the panel returned, `git worktree list` showed no new
`agent-*` worktree. The plan is gitignored (`docs/`), so its revision is
local-only; this file is the tracked record.
