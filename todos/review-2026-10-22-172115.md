- title: printf | grep -q skips the fields leg under a caller's pipefail

**Problem:**
`printf '%s\n' "$blocks" | grep -q '^H' || return 0`: grep -q exits on the first row, printf takes SIGPIPE once the rows pass the pipe buffer, and under `pipefail` the `|| return 0` reads that as "no findings". Reproduced independently by security-sentinel, silent-failure-hunter and adversarial-reviewer (600-3000 findings: N MISS plain, 0 under pipefail).

**Fix:**
Here-string (`grep -q '^H' <<<"$blocks"`). Control `field-pipefail` (1500 findings, gap at the end, sourced under `set -euo pipefail`); mutant restoring the pipe is killed by it.
